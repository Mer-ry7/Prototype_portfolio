SkillProof helps you prove your real skills with short practical tests, not only with words on a CV.

What is SkillProof?

SkillProof is a platform concept for people who want to show what they can really do. Instead of writing a list of skills on a CV, the user takes a short practical test, gets a verified score, and can share it with companies.

How the app works

  1. Choose a skill, for example HTML or CSS.
  2. Take a short practical test.
  3. Get a score out of 100 and a level.
  4. Share the result with a company.

Main objectives

The goal of SkillProof is to give beginners a fair way to show their level, and to help companies check real skills quickly. It also helps people see what they still need to learn.
